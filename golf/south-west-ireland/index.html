Where to stay: South West Ireland

Kerry, Cork and Limerick hold many of Ireland's most famous links, from Ballybunion, Tralee and Waterville to the Old Head of Kinsale, plus the 2027 Ryder Cup venue at Adare Manor. Peak-season tee times go early.
