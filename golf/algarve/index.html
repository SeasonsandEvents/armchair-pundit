Where to stay: Algarve

Portugal's southern coast is Europe's best-known winter golf destination. The busiest cluster lies around Quinta do Lago, Vale do Lobo and Vilamoura, within an hour of Faro airport, with quieter and often cheaper rounds to the east towards Spain and to the west around Portimão and Lagos.
