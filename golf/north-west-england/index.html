Where to stay: North West England

England's Golf Coast between Liverpool and Southport is lined with famous links, including Royal Birkdale and Royal Liverpool, with Royal Lytham a short drive north and quieter Cumbrian links beyond. Merseyrail trains run along much of the coast.
