Where to stay: Lisbon & the Silver Coast

Cascais and Sintra offer dune and pine golf within about forty minutes of Lisbon airport, while the Silver Coast around Óbidos, a little over an hour north, has some of Portugal's newer courses beside the Atlantic. Expect it to be cooler and breezier than the Algarve in winter.
