Where to stay: Costa del Sol

The coast from Málaga west past Marbella and Estepona to Sotogrande is so full of courses that it is often called the Costa del Golf, with Valderrama the most famous name. Málaga airport serves most of the coast; Gibraltar is closer for Sotogrande.
