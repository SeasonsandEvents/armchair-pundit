Where to stay: Northern Ireland

Lough Erne, the River Bush and dozens of lakes and rivers in the DAERA Public Angling Estate, open to visitors with a short-term licence and permit. Coarse anglers will find good bream and roach fishing on the Erne and the Lower Bann.
