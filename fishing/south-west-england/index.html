Where to stay: South West England

Moorland reservoirs, Devon and Cornish trout streams and the famous coarse rivers of Dorset and Hampshire. Many of the stillwaters here are run by South West Lakes Trust and Bristol Water and can be booked online, which makes a short break easy to plan.
