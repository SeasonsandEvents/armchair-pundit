Where to stay: Tayside & Central Scotland

The Tay and its tributaries, Loch Leven, Loch Tay and the lochs of the central belt. Association and town waters give an affordable way onto famous salmon rivers, and the trout lochs are easy to reach from Edinburgh and Glasgow.
